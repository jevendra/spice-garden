About Spice Garden

Serving Vijayawada a magical mix of authentic recipes, seasoned with passion, and served with unconditional warmth since 2014.

Spice Garden represents the culinary heritage of India. Our hand-crafted menu bridges traditional South Indian spices, North Indian tandoori clay-oven roasting, and high-intensity Chinese wok-tossed favorites. We source our whole spices from the organic spice hills of Andhra Pradesh and Kerala, ensuring every plate is packed with real flavor and aroma.

100% Fresh & Organic Ingredients

We never use artificial colors or MSGs. Our ingredients, oil, and meats are sourced fresh every morning.

Master & Experienced Chefs

Our culinary wizards carry decades of experience preparing dum biryanis and specialized tandoori platters.

Welcoming & Premium Atmosphere

An elegant interior styled with dark charcoal accents and warm lighting, perfect for families and celebrations.
